Join Willamette University's CT4Life Workshop Series

Shape the Future of TechNo Coding Required!

Have you ever felt like modern technology wasn't designed with your generation in mind? You're right — and we need your help to fix it.

The CT4Life (Computational Thinking for Life-Long Learners) project invites local Salem seniors to collaborate directly with Willamette University tech students. Together, we will co-design everyday technology solutions, tools, and problem-solving strategies tailored to real life. Your wisdom and lived experience are the most important tools in the room.

The Benefits

Why Participate?

Keep Your Mind Active

Try interactive, brain-stimulating activities that build critical thinking and everyday problem-solving skills.

Partner with Students

Work in small, friendly groups alongside energetic undergraduate tech guides eager to listen and learn from you.

Connect with Community

Meet fellow Salem neighbors who share your curiosity and passion for lifelong learning.

Enjoy Great Perks

Free food and drinks, a $10 gift card per workshop, and free bus transportation if you need a ride!

Mark Your Calendar

Workshop Details

Two Sessions

Wed, Sept 30 & Mon, Nov 16, 10:20–11:50 AM

Location

Willamette University Lecture Hall

What to Bring

Nothing! Just your curiosity and enthusiasm.

Getting Here

Free bus transportation available on request.

Is This For You?

Who Can Join

Adults aged 70 years or older (or 60+), living in the Salem area
Able to give consent and complete short conversational questionnaires
Willing to attend both 90-minute sessions and share feedback
Space Is Limited

Ready to Sign Up?

No prior technical experience or computer skills required — just bring your wisdom and curiosity.
