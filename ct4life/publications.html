2025 ACM SIGCSE Broadening Participation

Exploring Andragogy Principles for Broadening Participation in Computing Education for Lifelong Learners

Agbo, F. J., & Everetts, C. (2025). ACM Digital Library.

Computing education can foster older adults' cognitive abilities and create a community of active lifelong learners. This study examines how to design strategies to broaden participation in computing education for older adults based on Malcolm Knowles' andragogy principles, analyzing qualitative findings from 34 older adult participants (aged 60+) engaged in plugged and unplugged computational thinking workshops.

2024 ACM Inroads / SIGCSE Poster

Towards Computing Education for Lifelong Learners: Exploring Computational Thinking Unplugged Approaches

Agbo, F. J., & Everetts, C. (2024). ACM SIGCSE Technical Symposium.

Older adults are underrepresented in computing education; broadening participation has remained predominantly focused on K-12 and collegiate contexts. This empirical poster reports preliminary findings demonstrating that problem-oriented unplugged activities gently introduce older adults into computing while stimulating active social collaboration.

2024 Literature Review

Broadening Participation in Adult Education: A Literature Review of Computer Science Education

Agbo, F. J. (2024). ACM Conference on Computing Education Practice.

Systematic review of the past four decades of adult computing education literature across ACM Digital Library, Scopus, and Web of Science. Pinpoints critical gaps in adult learning outcomes and presents an actionable research roadmap for lifelong learner computing curricula.
