Educational Modules & Toolkits

Lessons & Co-Design Tools Accessible, Modular & Engaging

Step-by-step modular lessons and interactive toolkits developed for senior lifelong learners, educators, and undergraduate student guides.

Module 01 • Workshop 1

Demystifying Everyday Tech

An introductory conversation on how modern smart devices (smart TVs, automated checkouts, map navigators) process rules and steps. Includes the "Deconstruction of Everyday Tasks" guided worksheet.

Includes: Physical Worksheet & Cards
Module 02 • Workshop 1

Algorithmic Thinking & Flow

Interactive puzzle mapping where participants sequence step-by-step instructions for a "Robot Guide" (played by undergraduate student partners), learning conditional logic (If/Then) through humorous live demonstrations.

Includes: Roleplay Scenario Cards
Module 03 • Workshop 2

Visual Co-Design & Storycraft

Senior learners and students co-author interactive visual animations using large-button visual blocks. Express personal memories, historical timelines, or interactive trivia cards.

Includes: Large-Print Visual Interface
Module 04 • Workshop 2

User Interface Critique & Redesign

Participants act as "Chief Usability Consultants" to evaluate real consumer interfaces and propose redesigns for accessibility, high-contrast readability, and reduced cognitive load.

Includes: Co-Design Evaluation Rubric
For Facilitators & Students

Undergraduate Facilitation Guidelines

Our Willamette undergraduate tech guides are trained in empathy-first communication and adult learning dynamics before every workshop:

Listen First: Never grab the mouse or paper; let the senior participant drive every step at their own comfortable pace.
Avoid Jargon: Replace technical CS terms with relatable daily metaphors (e.g., recipe for algorithm, categorizing laundry for sorting).
Celebrate Wisdom: Recognize that older adults have decades of real-world problem solving that enriches every computing concept.
