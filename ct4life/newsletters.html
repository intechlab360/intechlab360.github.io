Workshop Series Preview

Welcoming Salem Seniors to Campus: The Fall Workshop Series Launches

We are thrilled to launch the Fall 2026 CT4Life Workshop Series at Willamette University! This semester, undergraduate students in Dr. Fred Agbo's Human-Computer Interaction and AI courses will pair with local community members (aged 60 and above) for two hands-on sessions in the campus Lecture Hall.

Highlights this term include new unplugged pattern puzzles, everyday digital interface critique, complimentary refreshments, free transit passes, and a $10 gift card per session for every participant.

Key Dates: Session 1: Wednesday, Sept 30, 10:20 AM – 11:50 AM
Session 2: Monday, Nov 16, 10:20 AM – 11:50 AM

Issue #1 • Spring Recap May 2026
Research Findings

"I Can Think Algorithmically Too!" — Voices from Our Spring Pilot Cohort

Following our 34-participant pilot study published at ACM SIGCSE, older adults expressed high satisfaction with unplugged problem-solving. Over 92% of participants reported heightened confidence in navigating modern digital interfaces, noting that working with enthusiastic Willamette students eliminated tech anxiety.

"I realized that computer science isn't just typing mysterious code into a dark screen. It's the same systematic planning I've used my whole life in gardening and household budgeting."
— Margaret D., Salem Community Participant (Age 74)
