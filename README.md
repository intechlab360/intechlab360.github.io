# iNTech Research Lab & CT4Life Project Portal

Official website for the **iNTech (Interactive Technology for Learning) Research Lab** at Willamette University, directed by Dr. Fred Agbo, including the dedicated **CT4Life** (Computational Thinking for Life-Long Learners) project website.

## 🌐 Live Website & Structure

- **Main Lab Portal:** [index.html](index.html)
  - Research themes & categorized project repositories (CT4Life, Immersive VR, Teacher-in-the-Loop GenAI, Learning Analytics, Inclusive STEM).
  - Selected peer-reviewed publications & research focus areas.
  - Lab team members and PI profile.
- **CT4Life Project Subsite:** [ct4life/index.html](ct4life/index.html)
  - [ct4life/activities.html](ct4life/activities.html) — Plugged and unplugged computational thinking learning activities.
  - [ct4life/publications.html](ct4life/publications.html) — Peer-reviewed papers, ACM SIGCSE publications, and grant support.
  - [ct4life/lessons.html](ct4life/lessons.html) — Lessons, co-design toolkits, and facilitation guides.
  - [ct4life/newsletters.html](ct4life/newsletters.html) — Community updates and participant spotlights.
  - [ct4life/adverts.html](ct4life/adverts.html) — Full interactive & print-ready workshop flyer and recruitment adverts.
